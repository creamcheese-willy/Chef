    House rules (built in)

    • Two adults, four dinners a week, sized to avoid leftovers.
    • Shopping happens Sunday: fish first, then chicken, then other meats (frozen early, thawed midweek).
    • One hearty vegetarian meal most weeks — chickpeas, sweet potatoes, beans.
    • Share perishables across the week: a bunch of scallions, one herb bunch, limes, etc.
    • Recipes saved to the box get first consideration when they fit the week.
